The Fabric Journey
Six frames. One feel.

Before
The Full Shelf
Cotton interlock is everywhere — every import catalog, every online supplier. The construction is not rare. What's rare is the verified version: organic, SustainSafe, Made in USA, certified. A full shelf that can't answer: what are you, really?
Face 1
Both Sides
Two needle beds built this fabric simultaneously — identical smooth surfaces on face and back. No wrong side. No hidden face. Every certification holds from both directions. Turn the fabric over. Still pure.
"Both sides are smooth and identical — difficult to distinguish right from wrong side."
Face 2
After the Wash
Hot water. Standard detergent. The organic cotton fibers compact — 7–8% length, 3–4% width. The hand shifts from soft to butter. Not a metaphor. A maker's word for what cotton becomes when you give the fibers permission to tighten. Every wash after is the same or better.
"Washed up beautifully. Has great body." — customer review
Face 3
The Ease
Edges lie flat — no curl. Standard machine tension — no adjustment. Straight stitch works for most construction. A first-time knit sewer felt confident before the needle touched the cloth. The most beginner-friendly knit in the ProECO collection.
"Gorgeous to touch and to sew." — customer review
Face 4
Still Pure
100% organic cotton. No synthetic fiber. No Lycra. No chemical finish. No coatings. The purity is not a feature — it's what remains when everything unnecessary has been removed. The feel at wash one hundred is the feel at wash one.
"I love Wazoodle and appreciate their dedication to organic USA products." — customer review
Highlight · Sid's Layer
SustainSafe Processing
Chemical-free manufacturing removes what the process left behind. pH 7.4 neutral — no acid, no base against the skin. No manufacturing residues. The processing doesn't add a feature. It removes everything that isn't cotton.
