A page here is a sequence of neighbourhoods on stepping surfaces, written in two typefaces, coloured by one brand at a time, with exactly one deep band, one buy colour and one spark. Every value on it is a token, so the same page becomes Zorb, ProSoft or SupAbsorb by changing one attribute. The forty records behind this live in our design system, linked from every card; the corrections written on them are the part a designer should read first.

Question one

What does everything we make have to look like?

Calm, warm, exact, and honest about what it is. These are the visual laws that enforce it — with the correction that earned each one on a live page.

Surfaces step. They never repeat.Paper → ground → stone → wash → deep. Two adjacent bands never share a ground.

The alternation is the page's rhythm. The seam between neighbourhoods is a line — nothing more. On the first Shopify deploy the seam labels ("District exit · toward the Decision") leaked our own architecture into customer view and had to be stripped: district names are structure, not copy. Band padding came down from 104px to 72px in the same audit; each over-generous boundary was a place a reader decided to stop.

One deep band a page.Depth is rationed so it lands. It carries the science; it is the only place the spark appears.

Three thin strata step down into it — 10, 14, 18px — so the reader falls rather than cuts. At 22px each they read as a glitch banner. The deep band is the page's one immersive moment; a second one is not more depth, it is less.

Red means you can act here. Nothing else.The Buy Box and the Sticky Bar. Never emphasis, never warning, never a brand.

This single law is why the pages feel calm. It generalises: every family of surfaces names its one colour of consequence and fences it. When the stock cluster needed a third status colour in July, the answer was not red and not the spark — it was a new goldenrod for exactly one job, Low stock, because a status colour must mean the same thing on every brand and the spark changes colour with the world.

The spark appears once per view.The one earned pop. Its default home is the hero number in the science band.

If it appears twice, remove one. Energy comes from rationing, not decoration — no gradients on backgrounds but a six-per-cent radial glow on the masthead, no neon, no emoji, no glow effects. Borders define; they don't shout.

Prose is 68 characters wide. Always.Density governs measure. Widen grids and media to fill a screen — never body text.

Four measures: reading at 68ch, invariant; instruments at 640–760 (Arch's letter, the FAQ, a single-variant spec table — the narrowness is the signal you're reading an artefact); the column at 940; the breakout to 1440 for grids of four or more, partner sets, voice walls. A two-column table given breakout width reads as a stretched table. The breakout itself was inert for weeks because a neutralising rule with !important beat every rule after it — when a fix doesn't take, suspect specificity before cache.

Two typefaces, jobs fixed.Newsreader for headlines, the fabric's italic, and prices. Hanken Grotesk for everything functional.

The eyebrow — Hanken 800, uppercase, tracked 0.18em — is the system's editorial signature and opens every module. It shipped at 12px in a light grey that measured 2.4:1 against white and nearly vanished on the wash band; the walk raised it to 13px and made its colour surface-aware. A documented "never deviate" rule that fails legibility changes when a live page proves it. Newsreader italic in voice green — the fabric speaking — is rationed like the spark: at most twice a page.

Every value is a token.One machine truth for all visual values. A raw hex in UI code fails design QA.

Components reference only the roles — signature, wash, deep, spark, surface, ink — never a brand's hex. That is the whole reason one attribute can re-skin a page: the roles stay fixed while the identity beneath them swaps. Semantic aliases sit between the two so a component never needs to know which brand is live.

Name the room, not the furniture.A module is named for what the visitor does there, never for what it's made of.

The Choosing, not "variant selector". The Keeping, not "care instructions". The Full Truth, not "disclaimers". Thirty-four rooms in seven neighbourhoods, and a designer reads the name and knows what the visitor is doing in it. The library reopens whenever the walk finds a room the theory missed.

Before it ships

Nine questions. All nine have to be yes.

  1. Do adjacent bands step surfaces, with exactly one deep band? Paper never touches paper; the deep band is singular.
  2. Is red only on a buy action? Anywhere else, it's a failure — including warnings.
  3. Does the spark appear once? If twice, remove one.
  4. Is every colour a token — no raw hex, no brand hex in a component? Switch the world; if anything doesn't follow, it wasn't a token.
  5. Is prose at 68 characters, and are grids, not paragraphs, what got wider? Empty space is answered with media and cards, never with longer lines.
  6. Is the eyebrow Hanken 800, uppercase, tracked — and legible on every surface it sits on? Check contrast per surface, not once.
  7. Is Newsreader only on headlines, the fabric's line and the price — and the italic used at most twice? If everything speaks in the serif, nothing does.
  8. Are the radii 999, 16, 12 or 6 — and the shadows warm, not grey? Pills for buttons and chips; 12 for inputs; 16 for cards.
  9. Was it checked on a phone and a laptop, and is every animation off under reduced motion? Desktop-correct and mobile-correct are different states.

And the one under all nine: does it feel calm, warm, exact — and would it still if you took the photography away?

For our side

Where the tokens moved, and where the truth is.Parrot's forty records are canonical. The build tokens were reconciled against them for this page.

Three things drifted between the July records and the September build tokens and are now aligned to Parrot: the eyebrow (13px, --muted, surface-aware — not 12px --label), the mono floor (11px minimum after the chamber tags rendered at 9px), and the width scale (breakout verified to 1440 on the live page, not capped at 1180). Two things Parrot still calls open and this page doesn't pretend to close: the type scale has grown to forty-two sizes across the product pages and is to be consolidated in one deliberate pass after the third fabric, not before; and seven primitives — accordion, table, modal, tabs, select, stepper, toast — are built from foundations when needed, never imported. The Director's Cut harness that lets this page switch worlds is the same mechanism lifted out of the ProSoft product-page prototype; the chakra chassis for the other estate shares the spine and borrows nothing else.

Coming home

You've seen the system. Here's the one thing it's for.

The system is the calm.

Fence the red, ration the spark, step the surfaces, hold the prose at 68 — and the page is quiet enough that the fabric can be heard. Every law here is a way of taking something away.

The world is one attribute.

Nothing about a Zorb page is designed twice. The same rooms, the same type, the same components — with the dye changed. If a design only works in one world, it isn't on the system yet.

The corrections are the canon.

Every rule here was tested on a live page and several were overturned there. Read the note on the record before the rule on the record. The walk corrects the theory, and the canon is updated rather than the page reverted.

The room is named for what she does in it.

Design the Choosing, the Keeping, the Full Truth — not a selector, a care block, a disclaimer. The names are the brief.
