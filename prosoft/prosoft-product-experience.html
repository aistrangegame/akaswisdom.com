Highlight · The Bond
ECO-Thermal Bonding. Fusion, not adhesive.
Heat fuses the TPU film to the polyester interlock — no adhesive between the layers. Nothing to dissolve. Nothing to degrade. Independently tested 50% stronger than competitor US-made PUL. The bond IS the fabric.
"A bond that was glued can be unglued. A bond that was fused cannot."
